FAQs

Questions? Look here

How long does a project take?

A logo usually takes 1–2 weeks, packaging 2–3 weeks. Bigger identity projects are scheduled after the brief.

Do you work with clients outside Karanganyar?

Yes. Most projects run fully online over WhatsApp and video calls, anywhere in Indonesia.

What files will I receive?

Print-ready PDF and AI files, PNG/JPG for digital use, and dielines for packaging. Source files are included in the bigger packages.

How does payment work?

50% down payment to start, 50% before the final files are sent. Bank transfer and e-wallets are accepted.

Can you help with printing?

Yes. I prepare the files to your printer’s specs and can recommend printers around Solo Raya.

You have different questions?

Send me a message and I’ll get back to you, usually within one working day.
